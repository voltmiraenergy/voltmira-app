Where do the solar numbers come from?

Yearly and monthly yield come from PVGIS, the European Commission's satellite-derived solar database, for the exact coordinates of the roof. Tariff schemes are applied automatically per market. Every assumption is printed on the proposal.

Why show a pessimistic number? Won't it scare clients?

The opposite, in practice. A range with visible assumptions reads as engineering; a single rosy number reads as sales. When your worst case still beats their electricity bill, the deal defends itself.

Does it work for Moldova's net billing?

Yes, that's home turf. Moldova's low export price makes self-consumption the whole game, so the engine models it month by month and values battery storage correctly.

What does a tracked proposal mean for my client's privacy?

The link records opens, time on page and interactions with the quote itself. Nothing else. No ad trackers, no profiling, EU-hosted.
