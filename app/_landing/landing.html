Questions installers ask

Fair questions.

Where do the solar numbers come from?+

Yearly and monthly yield come from PVGIS, the European Commission’s satellite-derived solar database, for the exact coordinates of the roof. Tariff schemes (net metering in Romania, net billing in Moldova, feed-in in Germany) are applied automatically per market. Every assumption is printed on the proposal.

Why show a pessimistic number? Won’t it scare clients?+

The opposite, in practice. Clients have heard too many perfect promises. A range with visible assumptions reads as engineering; a single rosy number reads as sales. When your worst case still beats their electricity bill, the deal defends itself.

Does it work for Moldova’s net billing?+

Yes — that’s home turf. Moldova’s low export price makes self-consumption the whole game, so the engine models it month by month, values battery storage correctly, and supports MDL display and Russian/Romanian proposals.

What does “tracked proposal” mean for my client’s privacy?+

The link records opens, time on page, and interactions with the quote itself — nothing else. No ad trackers, no profiling, EU-hosted. It’s the courtesy of knowing your quote was received, done transparently.
