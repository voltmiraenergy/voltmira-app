# First-Run Onboarding (build this into the app)

Goal: **first proposal link sent within 10 minutes of signup.**

Step 1 — Company (30s): name, logo upload, default market. Pre-filled from signup.
Step 2 — Your numbers (60s): €/kW you actually charge, battery price.
         Copy: "These drive every quote. Change them anytime in Settings."
Step 3 — First quote (3 min): guided — address → (PVGIS fetches real yield,
         show "Using real solar data for this location ✓") → size slider →
         instant three-band payback.
Step 4 — The magic moment: "Send it to yourself" — installer texts the
         proposal link to their own phone, opens it, and watches the
         "Opened · 12s viewed" event appear live on their dashboard.
         THIS is the moment they understand the product. Optimize everything
         toward it.

Follow-ups (email, via Resend):
- Day 1: 90-second Loom — "Send your first real proposal"
- Day 3: "Your widget" — embed snippet + where to paste it
- Day 7: if no proposal sent → "Want a 15-min setup call?" (founder does these)
